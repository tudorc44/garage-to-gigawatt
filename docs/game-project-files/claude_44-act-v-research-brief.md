# 44: Research brief: the third pass, for Act V's content pack (design thread, 10 Oct 2026)

**What this is:** the brief for one research dossier (to become doc 45). It grounds the designed values in doc 43 (Act V, "Firm", 2036-2040, approved 10 Oct 2026) before its content pack is written. Doc 43 §16 lists the gaps; this brief turns them into questions.

**Who runs it:** a research session with web access, as docs 31, 37 and 40 were produced. Not the build thread.

**Read first (in the G2G project):** doc 43 (the design: especially §6.2 futures, §7 plants, §8 the queue, §11 finance and valuation, §15.2 the exit), doc 40 (the second pass; don't redo what it settled) and doc 37 (the energy dossier).

## Rules (as docs 31, 36 and 40)
- **Grade every claim:** [A] primary or official source; [B] reputable reporting or company statement; [C] analyst, forecast or industry estimate; [D] inference, or designed.
- Mark **inference** separately from sourced facts.
- **Give ranges with their basis** when sources disagree, and say which you prefer and why.
- **Date everything** (publication date and date accessed).
- **Never invent a figure.** If a number isn't found, say so and give the closest proxy, labelled.
- **Real company names are reference points only.** The game uses fictional names. Report what real companies did; don't predict their outcomes.
- **For every question, end with a correction table:** doc 43's designed value | what the sources say | grade | recommended game value. Use the game's units ($/kW, $/MW-yr, x EBITDA, bp over SOFR, quarters, %). Show the working for any conversion.
- Keep it short: about the length of doc 40. Depth where the numbers are, not on background.

## Questions, in priority order

### Q1. What operating power plants sell for (blocks §11.3, the plant multiple)
Doc 43 values an operating plant at EBITDA × 12 when contracted and × 7 when merchant (designed), and a fund buys stakes at value × 0.90-1.15 by future.
- EV/EBITDA (or EV/MW where EBITDA isn't disclosed) in transactions for operating plants, 2015-2026: contracted renewables, operating nuclear, geothermal, gas (contracted and merchant), battery storage, pumped hydro. Separate contracted from merchant where possible.
- How multiples moved with interest rates (2021 vs 2023-26).
- Infrastructure-fund and yieldco buyers: typical premiums or discounts to listed peers; any data on how much more a fully contracted plant fetches than a merchant one.
- What the game needs: a contracted and a merchant multiple per plant class, and a range for the fund bid index.

### Q2. Project finance for operating plants (blocks §11.1, plant debt)
Doc 43: up to 60% of plant value, SOFR + 250 bp (≥ 70% contracted) or + 400 bp (merchant), 15-year amortising, non-recourse; lenders take the plant after 2 quarters below DSCR 1.0.
- Typical debt sizing (debt/value or DSCR targets) and spreads for operating contracted renewables, nuclear (operating-plant refinancings), geothermal, storage and merchant gas, 2020-2026.
- Tenors and amortisation; what happens on a DSCR breach (cash sweep, lock-up, step-in) and how often lenders actually take a plant.
- What the game needs: leverage cap, spread by contracted share, tenor, the breach sequence.

### Q3. The interconnection queue (blocks §8)
Doc 43: waits by region from the future's file (24 quarters in 2036, moving to 14-28 by future); paying network upgrades cuts the remaining wait by a third at a designed $150/kW; positions move only with their site; a fast lane for curtailable load (V2) connects in 4-8 quarters with up to 200 hours a year of curtailment.
- Network upgrade costs per kW by RTO (LBNL's interconnection cost studies for PJM, MISO, SPP, ERCOT, CAISO, NYISO, ISO-NE), for large loads where available, and their trend.
- Queue waits and withdrawal rates by region, latest data (2024-2026), for generation and, separately, large loads.
- Does paying for upgrades actually shorten waits? Any evidence (surplus interconnection, energy-only service in ERCOT, "connect and manage", provisional service).
- Can a queue position be transferred, and on what terms (with the project, with the site, or on its own) in PJM, ERCOT, SPP, MISO and CAISO?
- Status of large-load reform since doc 40 (FERC's June 2026 show-cause orders and the RTO responses; DOE's 60-day study for curtailable loads): what has been adopted, and the curtailment terms (hours a year, notice) in any approved flexible-load tariff.
- The market price of "powered land" or sites with a queue position or signed interconnection ($/MW), 2023-2026: what buyers paid for the position itself.
- What the game needs: upgrade $/kW by region; wait and withdrawal bands; the transfer rule; the fast lane's speed and curtailment terms; a queue premium in $/kW.

### Q4. Demand beyond 2035 (blocks §6.2, the AI demand index)
IEA's cases stop at 2035 (700-1,700 TWh for data centres [C]).
- Any published US or global data-centre or AI electricity forecasts that reach 2040 (EPRI, LBNL, BNEF, Grid Strategies, utilities' IRPs, McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, IEA WEO long-term cases), with their range across cases.
- US total load growth forecasts to 2040 (NERC LTRA, utilities, FERC filings).
- What the game needs: a 2036-2040 path per future (V1 high, V2 high-mid, V3 plateau near the low case, V4 mid with a share moving to orbit), with each anchor graded.

### Q5. Capacity accreditation for firm plants (blocks §7.1)
- PJM's ELCC class ratings (current and preliminary to 2035/36) for nuclear, gas combined cycle, combustion turbines, and long-duration storage (8-10 h); any equivalent in MISO or ERCOT's proposals.
- What the game needs: an accreditation factor per firm class by year.

### Q6. Operating-plant economics (blocks §7.1, §7.3)
- Fixed O&M ($/kW-yr) for SMRs (vendor estimates and NREL ATB), large nuclear, enhanced geothermal, pumped hydro, gas CC and CT, iron-air or other long-duration storage.
- The typical contracted share of output for new nuclear, geothermal and storage projects (how much is sold forward at COD).
- Peak-off-peak price spreads by region (PJM, ERCOT, CAISO) 2022-2026, and the outlook, for pumped storage's arbitrage margin.
- What the game needs: fixed costs per class, a contracted share at COD per class, a spread per region.

### Q7. Exit benchmarks (blocks §15.2)
Doc 43: a strategic buyer bids the company's valuation × 0.95-1.15 by future (designed); "hold" is marked by a hidden post-2040 outlook factor (designed [D]).
- Control premiums in acquisitions of data-centre, power and infrastructure companies, 2015-2026 (premium to the undisturbed price or to the last private mark).
- Recent data-centre and power-platform take-privates and sales (EV/EBITDA or EV/MW), as reference points.
- What the game needs: a bid-factor range with its basis. The outlook factor stays designed; say only whether anything published bounds it.

### Q8. Policy and opposition after 2035 (doc 40 gaps; blocks §6 and the wildcards)
- Federal clean-electricity credit phase-outs as now legislated (the 45Y/48E schedule after the 2025 law), and whether nuclear, geothermal and storage keep credits past 2035.
- State 2035/2040 clean-power targets in the regions the game uses (Texas, PJM states, Georgia, Arizona, Ohio) and the Nordics.
- Federal nuclear policy beyond 2026 (licensing timelines, any targets for new capacity by 2050).
- Community opposition to data centres and large loads after 2026: moratoria, local bans, polling trends.
- Large power-transformer lead times (2022-2026) and the outlook (basis for the transformer-shortage wildcard).
- What the game needs: which policies switch on or off in 2036-2040 in every future (the known timeline), and a basis for the moratorium and transformer wildcards.

### Q9. Quick verifications
- Bitcoin's 2036 and 2040 halvings: block-height estimates and likely quarters at current block times.
- NIST's post-quantum timeline: any change since IR 8547's draft (deprecate after 2030, disallow after 2035).
- Fusion: any device with net electricity (not scientific gain) announced by October 2026? Confirms or breaks the "nothing before 2038" honesty rule.
- SMR orderbook status since doc 40 (units under construction vs announced, worldwide), for the SMR-wave decoy's basis.
