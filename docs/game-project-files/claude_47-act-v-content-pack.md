## 7. Open questions for the design thread

1. **Carrack or Pallas.** Doc 46 lists "Pallas Compute"; the game shows Carrack Compute since M34.3. The pack uses Carrack
   (assumption 31). Confirm.
2. **Scarcity-neutral columns.** The pack holds rents, renewal and RFP indices, the AI multiple and the old 0-100 demand
   index flat and relies on the engine to apply `scarcity_index` (assumption 4). The build should list every carried
   system that reads those columns and confirm each applies the factor; otherwise those systems won't feel the futures.
3. **The capacity price baseline.** The median rule gives $432.7/MW-day in 2036, above PJM's real $325 cap; pumped
   storage then earns ≈ $114/kW-yr of capacity, not doc 45's ≈ $85 (doc 43 §0.6 fixed $85 for the gate). Options: (a)
   keep the median and accept $114; (b) rebase Act V's capacity column to $325 (batteries' capacity income would drop 25%
   at the seam); (c) keep the column and cap pumped capacity revenue at $85 × the future's capacity factor. *Pack
   recommends (c): it keeps the seam smooth and the gate's pumped economics.* **This is where the pack departs from doc
   43**: §0.4's formula and §0.6's flat $85 can't both hold with the game's own capacity price.
4. **The gate's V1 stub rose from 2036Q1;** V-B14 forces 2036 flat, so V1's scarcity gain is slightly smaller than the
   gate measured. Worth a re-run of M43.0d on these files (no tuning expected).
5. **The exit bid in 2040Q3 reveals part of the future** (V1's range sits above V3's). Intended by doc 43 §15.2 (a known
   bid), but it also hints at the outlook factor's direction. Keep, or show the same 1.05-1.25 range in every future and
   draw the future-specific factor inside it?
6. **CC and CT ratings:** doc 43 §0.4 reads as a ramp over the act; PJM's preliminary set already has 0.78 and 0.70 by
   2035/36 [A]. Flat 0.78 / 0.70 is the sourced reading.
7. **Withdrawing a queue position** isn't in doc 43's move table; the pack scores it 0. It arguably reduces exposure (−1).
8. **V2's flexible payments.** Doc 43 doesn't say what an enrolled flexible MW earns in Act V outside Texas. Without a
   payment, V-B3 (Flex Operator ≥ 1.15 × Holder) rests only on the fast lane's speed. Add a designed capacity payment for
   enrolled MW in V2 (§4)?
9. **The clean-credit cliff.** The game has never modelled the federal production credit, so "plants started by 2033 keep
   theirs" changes nothing in play. Keep it as a card only (pack's choice), or model 45Y at ~$30/MWh for 10 years
   (verify) for plants started by 2033?
10. **The seam glide and bitcoin.** The 2036Q1 halving halves hashprice on Act V's first quarter. The glide should skip the
    subsidy, hashprice and the capacity ratings (protocol and rule facts, identical in every Act IV future anyway).
11. **Orrery and "Start at Act V".** Orrery appears only for players arriving from F1 or F3; the presets should carry the
    Act IV future of the run that made them.
12. **Weekly files gain six power columns** (Act IV's weekly had none): a loader schema change for the build.
13. **Megawatt titles:** Firm, Flexible, Orbital, Landlord read well; "Firm" doubles as "company", a pun worth keeping.
    Alternative to Landlord if a fourth adjective is wanted: "Leased".
14. **Lunar holding:** confirm it reads only `lunar_value_index` × its 2035Q4 value (the lunar price columns stay flat).
15. **Fusion pilot running costs** aren't in any doc; the pack borrows the reactor lines. A research gap.
16. **Doc 43 §0.2's "holding a position costs $50/kW of security"** is in `plants_v.json` → `queue`; confirm it is a deposit
    (returned on energisation) rather than a cost.
